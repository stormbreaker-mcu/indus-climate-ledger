## Part 2: Structured Academic Abstract

### **Title:** 
The Climate-Resilience Ledger: Reinterpreting the Indus Script as an Ecological Survival Technology

### **Abstract:**
The undeciphered script of the Indus Valley Civilization (IVC) (c. 2600–1900 BCE) is traditionally interpreted as a phonetic writing system. We propose an ecological and administrative framework that reinterprets the Indus script as a non-linguistic "Climate-Resilience Ledger"—a administrative technology designed to manage severe resource scarcity during the 4.2 ka mega-drought.

**Keywords:** Indus Script; 4.2 ka Mega-Drought; Palaeoclimatology; Ancient Sanitation; Archaeological Simulation.
